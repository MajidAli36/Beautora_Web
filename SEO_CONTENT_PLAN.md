# Beautora SEO Content Plan

## Positioning

**Beautora — AI-Powered Salon Management Software**  
Global category: AI-powered salon & beauty-business management platform.  
Pakistan = important market (JazzCash, directory), not the brand identity.

## AI honesty

- Live: AI-assisted marketing help (optional), strong operational automation.
- Not live: autonomous voice AI phone receptionist.
- Pages `/salon-ai` and `/ai-receptionist` state this clearly.

## Publish now (unique content)

Commercial hubs, industry pages, markets: USA, UK, UAE, Pakistan, blog seed posts (2), tools (2 calculators).

## Do not mass-generate

City doorway pages, fake comparisons, thin country clones, AI article farms.

## Next content backlog

1. More blog posts from real operator pain (inventory waste, retail attach rate).
2. UAE/Saudi Arabic landing **only** with true translation.
3. Factual `/compare/beautora-vs-*` when feature matrices are verified.
4. Additional calculators (break-even, ROI) as real interactive tools.
5. City pages only with genuine local demand + unique copy.
